# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Hookbase is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone who participates in the project.

We expect contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community members to communicate professionally and respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent while discussing disagreements.
- Focus criticism on code, APIs, and ideas rather than people.
- Welcome different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Sexualized comments or unwanted advances.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Repeated bad-faith participation.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make the project unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to a project maintainer through the security/contact channel documented in `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may remove content, close issues or pull requests, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official Hookbase community spaces.
